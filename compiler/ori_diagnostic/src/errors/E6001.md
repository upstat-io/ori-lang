# E6001: Division by Zero

A checked integer-backed division - `int`, `byte`, `Duration / int`, or
`Size / int` - used zero as its divisor. Floating-point division does not
trigger this code.

## Fix

Check the divisor before dividing and handle the zero case explicitly.
